A public archive of research notes from **Applied Decision Intelligence** on reinforcement learning, sequential decision-making, Bayesian methods, operations research, and applied decision systems.

The purpose of this repository is to keep conceptual and research-oriented notes separate from code-heavy reproducible research projects.

## Research Notes

### 01. How Reinforcement Learning Fits into Quantitative Investing

**Subtitle:** *From prediction to adaptive portfolio decisions*

A conceptual research note on where reinforcement learning can add value beyond prediction and conventional portfolio optimization. The article focuses on sequential decisions, constrained portfolio adjustment, uncertainty-aware action, and the option to make no change when the evidence is weak.

[Read the GitHub research note](./01-how-rl-fits-into-quantitative-investing/)

The folder includes:

- the full PDF article;
- a short research summary;
- citation information;
- the article cover.
